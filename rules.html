THE RULES


OVERVIEW OF GAME

For the entire week, we'll be playing a social deduction game called Mafia.

During this game, everyone will be part of a faction, Good, Evil or Neutral, and have to use their abilities and the gallows to secure their faction a victory.

Whenever someone dies, their role is automatically revealed.

If you wish, you can DM me a will at any time, which will be shared once you die. please start the message with "WILL:" and pin it. You can edit it whenever you die.


WIN CONDITIONS

The Good faction wins when all players from the Evil faction are dead.

The Evil faction wins when the amount of players in the Evil faction is the same, or greater than the amount of players outside of the Evil factions.

All Neutral players have their own win condition they need to achieve to gain the prize alongside the winning team.

Although, if ALL of the Neutral players achieve their win conditions, the game ends early and the Neutral players win the channel.


PLAYING THE GAME

Each day will be split into three parts:


DAYTIME

lasts 23 hours, during which you can DM me the name of a player you want to nominate for the gallows.

In order for a player to become a nominee, they must get at least 3 votes.

You can nominate up to three players in my DMs to be sent off to the gallows.

THE NOMINATION

If there's at least one nominee, they are sent off to the gallows.

A nominee needs 3 votes to be nominated for the gallows.

If there are no nominees that get three or more votes, nothing happens.

If there are ten or more nominees, only the nine with the most votes are eligible for the gallows.

THE GALLOWS

The nomination ends at THISISWHEREYOUPUTTHETIME, then the final vote begins.

The nominees will have the ability to defend themselves during the vote, whilst everyone else votes which of them to kill in a discord poll, lasting 25 minutes.

You can also vote to skip the vote

If the vote succeeds, the player with the most votes is killed.

Afterwards there will be a 5 minute break before the night.

NIGHT - PHASE ONE

During the night, the server will be locked down for everyone playing the game.

Everyone with a night ability will have 15 minutes to submit their night actions in my DMs, their personal description includes the formatting of the night action.

The Mafia (Evil Faction), will also have to spend the 15 minutes on voting who they should kill during the night.

If your action isn't submtited in 15 minutes, it will not be considered.

NIGHT - PHASE TWO

I will have to figure out what to do with all of your night actions, and update the log in 15 minutes.

Once 15 minutes have passed, I will publish the site changes, and DAYTIME will begin again.




CODE OF CONDUCT

DON'T SHARE INFO

You're not allowed to share information about the game with other players while dead.

Any circumvention of this, such as sending it through a third party also counts as breaking the rules.

You're also not allowed to provide screenshots or stream DMs as evidence.

WHERE TO TALK

Refrain from plotting with people in DMs

If you're a mafia member, please try only talking with the rest of your team in #general-evil

As a ghost you're allowed to keep talking in #spam, but the alive people aren't allowed in there.

PUNISHMENTS

Breaking the rules can result in one of three things depending on severity and intent.

A verbal warning + loss of ability for a day/night cycle.

Removal from event, being killed off by The Host.

Repeated serious offences can end with an actual warning in the server, placing you on Last Chance.



Also, I think this doesn't need to be pointed out, but playing the event doesn't make it so that you can ignore the server's actual rules. Moderation will still be happening normally.



If there's anything you're unsure about regarding the rules, feel free to DM me about it.
